# Release 1.0.0 — 25 November 2026

Initial P4 nominal fixed-income research release. Includes 499 official 2024/2025 Treasury par curves; regular dated bond mathematics; transparent educational bootstrap; log-discount, linear-discount and Nelson-Siegel comparisons; duration, convexity and six key-rate buckets; equal-capital portfolios; bounded and scalar-neutral hedge comparisons; fixed-holding carry bridge; and retrospective no-change/PCA/ridge forecasts with costed hypothetical decision value.

Delivered review surfaces: eight executed notebooks, a 15-sheet formula-driven Excel review workbook, an eight-view interactive dashboard, an academic English paper, editable DOCX and two-page method note. TNR 12-point report typography and 1.5 body spacing are applied throughout.

Research is frozen through 31 December 2025. Market receipts refresh separately. Actual CUSIP quotes, legal holiday payment adjustments, odd stubs, credit/liquidity/default models, futures CTD and prospective performance remain outside this release. These are scope limits, not implemented features.

Validation comprises mathematical and temporal unit tests, historical curve/price/risk/ledger regeneration, independent Excel and JavaScript cases, native spreadsheet recalculation, document/layout inspection and release-file digests. Deployed CI and browser checks are recorded in the release review receipt after publication.
